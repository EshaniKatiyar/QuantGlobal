Q1: A stock price follows a geometric Brownian motion (GBM) with drift μ = 0.05 and volatility σ = 0.2. At time t = 0, the initial stock price is S0 = 100. 

What is the expected value of the stock price at time T, given that T is exponentially distributed with a rate parameter λ = 1?

Topic: Geometric Brownian Motion

Answer: 
E[S(T)] = S0 * e^(μ*T - (σ^2/2)*T)
