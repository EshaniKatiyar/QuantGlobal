Q[2]: A trading algorithm uses a moving average crossover strategy to generate buy and sell signals. The short-term moving average (MA) is set at 10 periods, while the long-term MA is set at 50 periods. If the short-term MA crosses above the long-term MA, the algorithm generates a buy signal. Otherwise, it generates a sell signal. Given a sample of daily returns for a stock over the past year, what is the probability that the algorithm will generate a buy signal?

Topic: Statistical Arbitrage and Moving Averages

Answer: 0.5 (assuming equal weightage to both signals)

Topic: Portfolio Optimization and Sharpe Ratio
